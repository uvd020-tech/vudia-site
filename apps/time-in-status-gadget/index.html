Time in Status + Gadget

How long every open issue has sat in its status, oldest first, right on your Jira dashboard.

Time in status for open work: how long each issue has been in progress, waiting in To Do or untouched, oldest first, flagged late or overdue against your team's own pace. A live dashboard gadget, not a history report.

Overview and user guide
A Jira dashboard gadget that lists the work nobody is moving: time in progress, time waiting to start or time since last update, against a threshold you set, plus a time-in-status panel on every issue.
Finding stuck issues in Jira with JQL
Five JQL queries that list the work nobody is moving, using nothing but Jira itself, and the point where a query stops being enough.
