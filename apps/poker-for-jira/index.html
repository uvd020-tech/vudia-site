Poker ++ for Jira

Real-time Planning Poker inside Jira: secret votes, story points or time estimates, round timers, saved straight to Jira.

Backlog estimation as a fast, secret-vote round. Cards stay hidden until the facilitator reveals them all at once, so the team sees where they disagree — and the agreed number goes straight into Jira.

View on Atlassian MarketplaceDocumentation

At a glance

  • Also called: Planning Poker for Jira
  • Jira Cloud app, built on Atlassian Forge
  • Runs on Atlassian infrastructure and makes no outbound network calls
  • Free for up to 10 users
