Cost Tracker ++ for Jira

Calculate Jira project cost from logged time and hourly rates, and track it against budget and revenue.

Cost Tracker takes the time your team already logs in Jira and multiplies it by hourly rates to get project cost. Add expenses and revenue, compare with the budget, and see profit for each project and across portfolios.
