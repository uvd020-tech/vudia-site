About Vudia

Vudia is a software studio building focused apps for teams that work in Jira.

Our goal is simple: solve real, everyday problems for agile and cross-functional teams with clean, intuitive tools that don't get in the way. No bloat, no unnecessary complexity, just apps that do their job well and are easy to set up.

Have feedback, a bug to report, or an idea for a feature? Reach out anytime — we read every message and use it to shape what we build next.

Our apps

  • Poker ++ for Jira: Real-time Planning Poker inside Jira: secret votes, story points or time estimates, round timers, saved straight to Jira.
  • Smart Defaults: Pre-fill the Jira Create dialog with default values for each project and issue type.
  • Cost Tracker ++ for Jira: Calculate Jira project cost from logged time and hourly rates, and track it against budget and revenue.
  • Time in Status + Gadget: How long every open issue has sat in its status, oldest first, right on your Jira dashboard.
