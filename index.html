About Vudia

Our goal is simple: solve real, everyday problems for agile and cross-functional teams with clean, intuitive tools that don't get in the way. No bloat, no unnecessary complexity, just apps that do their job well and are easy to set up. All our apps are Jira Cloud apps built on Atlassian Forge and listed on the Atlassian Marketplace.

Have feedback, a bug to report, or an idea for a feature? Reach out anytime — we read every message and use it to shape what we build next. Write to info@vudia.site. More about Vudia.
